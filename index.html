Does any of this sound familiar?

Signs that your marriage is being attacked...

Twisting of words, confusion, and gaslighting.

You speak with good intentions, yet somehow your words are always misunderstood. He hears things you never said. Your concerns are turned back on you. You leave conversations replaying every detail, wondering how everything became so distorted. Over time, the constant confusion, blame, and gaslighting can leave you feeling emotionally exhausted and questioning your own reality.

Explosive anger, rage, or frustration.

Everything seems to trigger frustration. Ordinary conversations become tense, and you never know what might set him off. You find yourself missing the man you first fell in love with—the one who was patient, gentle, and caring. Sometimes he explodes in anger, only to later deny it happened or claim he doesn't remember. Meanwhile, you're left walking on eggshells, wondering what changed and hoping tomorrow will somehow be different.

Signs that you're being attacked directly...

Anxiety, fear, panic, depression, and relentless negative thoughts can leave you feeling exhausted and defeated. But have you ever stopped to consider where those thoughts are coming from? Not every thought deserves your agreement. Learning to recognize the enemy's lies is the first step toward reclaiming the peace God intends for you.

Sleepless nights, racing thoughts, disturbing dreams, and a constant lack of rest can leave you physically, emotionally, and spiritually drained. Scripture shows that God gives His children peace and rest. If you feel like your sleep has become a battleground, you may be facing more than ordinary sleeplessness.

Unexpected setbacks, financial misfortune, self-destructive habits. It seems like the same destructive patterns keep repeating. You take one step forward then two steps back. You begin to wonder why life feels so unusually difficult and why the cycle never seems to end. While not every hardship has a spiritual cause, recurring patterns can point to a deeper battle taking place.

Signs that you're being attacked through other people...

Controlling behavior, manipulation, gaslighting, constant blame. Sometimes the greatest source of emotional exhaustion comes through the people closest to you—an in-law, family member, friend, or even a boss. They cross boundaries, then make you feel guilty for having them. Over time, you begin questioning your instincts, your judgment, and whether you're allowed to say "no" at all.

Rapid mood changes, constant indecision, contradictory words and actions that never seem to make sense. One moment they agree with you, the next they've completely changed their position. The enemy often uses confusion, instability, and deception to distract, divide, and keep God's people from walking in peace and discernment.
